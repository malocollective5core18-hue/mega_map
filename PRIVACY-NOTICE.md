# Location privacy notice / Ilani ya faragha ya eneo

**Notice version:** `2026-10-location-v1`
**Status:** Product copy for owner/legal review; not a legal approval.

## Kiswahili

Unapogusa **Endelea** kwenye hatua ya kuchagua eneo, kivinjari huomba kifaa chako kipime eneo mara moja kwa kutumia huduma ya eneo ya kifaa. Kipimo hudumu kwa takriban sekunde 15 na hukoma tunapopata kipimo, muda ukiisha, ukighairi, au ukiondoka kwenye hatua hiyo. Kivinjari kinaweza kukuuliza uruhusu eneo; ukikataa, unaweza kuweka pini mwenyewe na kuthibitisha eneo.

Tunatumia viwianishi vya kipimo, usahihi unaoripotiwa na kifaa, na muda wa kipimo. Ikiwa kifaa kinaripoti mwendo, tunatumia kasi hiyo kwenye kifaa pekee kukuomba usimame; kasi haitumwi wala kuhifadhiwa. Pini ya mwisho, chanzo cha eneo (GPS au pini ya mkono), umri wa kipimo unaokokotolewa na seva, umbali kati ya kipimo cha awali na pini ya mwisho, na kama pini ilihamishwa kwa mkono hutumwa na kuhifadhiwa ili timu iweze kupata biashara yako na kutathmini pini.

Hatuwezi kusoma vipimo ghafi vya satelayiti, Wi-Fi au minara ya simu kupitia ukurasa huu. Hatuendelei kufuatilia eneo lako baada ya kipimo ulichoanzisha. Kivinjari hakiwezi kuthibitisha kuwa mtu yupo kimwili hapo au kugundua kwa uhakika eneo bandia. GPS ni makadirio, si dhamana; tafadhali angalia pini kwenye mlango wa biashara yako. Taarifa zako kamili na viwianishi sahihi vinaonekana kwa wafanyakazi walioidhinishwa kwa huduma pekee. Biashara yako haitachapishwa kwenye ramani ya umma bila chaguo lako tofauti la hiari.

## English

When you tap **Continue** in the location step, the browser asks your device once for a fused location estimate. Capture lasts up to about 15 seconds and stops after a fix, timeout, cancellation, or leaving the step. Your browser may ask you to allow location access; if you decline, you can place the pin manually and confirm it.

We use the captured coordinates, device-reported accuracy, and fix time. If the device reports movement, speed is used on your device only to ask you to stop; speed is not sent or stored. The final pin, location source (GPS or manual pin), server-calculated fix age, distance from the original fix to the final pin, and whether you moved the pin by hand are sent and stored so the team can find your business and assess the pin.

This page cannot read raw satellite, Wi-Fi, or cell-tower measurements. We do not keep tracking your location after the capture you started. A browser cannot prove a person's physical presence or reliably detect a spoofed location. GPS is an estimate, not a guarantee; please check the pin at your business entrance. Your full details and exact coordinates are available only to authorized staff for service. Your business is not shown on the public map unless you separately choose the optional public listing.

## Data and limitations

- Customer location capture is a user-started, short-lived browser Geolocation API operation. The public map's separate **My location** control is also user initiated.
- Stored signal fields: selected/final latitude and longitude, reported accuracy when available, source, server-calculated fix age, pin offset, hand-moved flag, staff correction and its actor/time, plus the notice version and consent state.
- Not stored: the original GPS coordinate as a second point, motion stream, device speed, compass heading, Wi-Fi/cell identifiers, raw satellite readings, or background location history.
- The default service rectangle in environment configuration is broad and is not an authoritative Tanzania administrative/service boundary. The owner must approve and configure the actual service area before relying on it.
- Retention period, correction/deletion contact and statutory privacy language still require owner/legal decisions before live customer operation.
