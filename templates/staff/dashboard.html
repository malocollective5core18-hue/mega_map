SUPERUSER ONLY

Service teams

Build a service team from staff accounts already registered in the system, choose its provider lead, then assign the team to a customer who has submitted a location.

How teams work

The provider lead and selected members use their existing accounts and MFA. Add service providers and field team members to do the visit; add an admin assistant when the job needs admin support. Provider leads and field-team members see only customer locations assigned to their team. Admin assistants retain their usual admin-wide access. Team members use existing logins; no extra accounts are created.
