# Staff guide / Mwongozo wa wafanyakazi

## English

1. Open `/staff/` and sign in with the staff account created for you. On first sign-in, scan the authenticator QR or enter the manual setup key, then enter its six-digit code. Save the one-time recovery codes securely before continuing. Later sign-ins require an authenticator or an unused recovery code. Sign out on shared devices.
2. Dispatchers and admin assistants use **Customer map** and **Registered customers** to find and edit customer records, copy coordinates, and open map directions. Use **Flag customer** to select a category and add a short case note; open cases are stored in the profile history and audit log. Admin assistants can view customer-key history but cannot issue keys or create staff accounts.
3. Service providers open **Customer keys** to issue a reusable key for a new business profile and its phone. Every business profile needs its own key, even if two businesses share a phone. To issue or renew a key for an existing profile, a superuser starts from that customer’s Registered customers row so the key is linked to the right business. Search/filter your own issued keys and give the key to the matching customer. Choose **Revoke** in the key row and confirm to disable a key immediately. Copy a new key when it is shown; the secret cannot be retrieved later. The key works until its expiry or revocation.
4. Customers open the public map's hamburger menu or **Customer sign in / register**, then enter the phone and key. A new phone proceeds to registration. A known phone loads its saved details and location so the customer can update them.
5. Only the superuser can create admin assistants, service-provider accounts, and field-team accounts. Admin assistants cannot create other staff accounts or issue customer keys. Providers can issue keys but cannot access the customer directory. The superuser and admin assistants can review audit history and export data.
6. Use **Navigate · Google Maps** or another map app from a customer record. The link carries coordinates only. Call the customer before travelling when location confidence is low. Pin corrections are recorded with the staff account and time.
7. Field-team members see only jobs assigned to their account. Open a job and use **Check my position against this pin** for a one-time comparison. The browser asks for location permission when tapped; the page shows the approximate distance and device-reported accuracy. The check is not proof of presence and staff coordinates are not stored. Use the corrected-pin control only after checking the business entrance; corrections are audited.
8. Admin assistants and the superuser can use **Delete customer** at the bottom of a profile. Confirm carefully: deletion removes the profile, location, consent and visits, revokes linked customer keys, and cannot be undone. Keys linked to that profile are revoked; a legacy key that cannot be tied to just one profile for the same phone is revoked for safety. Separate keys linked to other businesses sharing the phone remain active. Audit and flag-case records are retained without the customer profile; do not put unnecessary personal information in flag notes.

**MFA and recovery:** TOTP enrollment, QR/manual setup, hashed single-use recovery codes, regeneration, throttling/lockout, audit events, and session/API enforcement are implemented on this branch and have SQLite tests. PostgreSQL and browser verification have not run. The MFA recovery owner must be named and `MFA_RECOVERY_OWNER_EMAIL` configured before staff account reenrollment can be performed; the reset API fails closed while it is blank. The Django `/admin/` site is restricted to superusers and requires the same verified staff session. Do not treat this branch as production-ready until the remaining release gates pass.

## Kiswahili

1. Fungua `/staff/` na uingie kwa akaunti uliyopewa. Mara ya kwanza, changanua QR ya programu ya uthibitishaji au andika ufunguo wa usanidi, kisha weka msimbo wake wa tarakimu sita. Hifadhi misimbo ya urejeshaji inayoonyeshwa mara moja mahali salama kabla ya kuendelea. Uingiapo tena, utahitaji programu ya uthibitishaji au msimbo wa urejeshaji ambao haujatumiwa. Toka kwenye akaunti ukitumia kifaa cha pamoja.
2. Dispatchers na wasaidizi wa admin hutumia **Customer map** na **Registered customers** kutafuta na kusahihisha taarifa za wateja, kunakili viwianishi na kufungua ramani ya maelekezo. Tumia **Flag customer** kuchagua aina ya tatizo na kuandika maelezo mafupi; kesi huhifadhiwa kwenye historia ya mteja na kumbukumbu ya ukaguzi. Wasaidizi wa admin wanaweza kuona historia ya funguo za wateja lakini hawawezi kutoa funguo wala kuunda akaunti za wafanyakazi.
3. Watoa huduma hufungua **Customer keys** kutoa ufunguo unaoweza kutumika tena kwa simu ya mteja. Tafuta au chuja funguo ulizotoa na umpe ufunguo mteja mwenye simu inayolingana. Bonyeza **Revoke** kwenye mstari wa ufunguo na uthibitishe ili kuuzima mara moja. Unapoonyeshwa, nakili ufunguo mpya; hauwezi kuonekana tena. Utaendelea kufanya kazi hadi muda wake uishe au uzuiwe.
4. Mteja afungue menyu ya mistari mitatu kwenye ramani ya umma au **Customer sign in / register**, kisha aweke simu na ufunguo. Simu mpya itaendelea kwenye usajili. Simu iliyosajiliwa itaonyesha taarifa na eneo lililohifadhiwa ili mteja aweze kusasisha.
5. Superuser pekee ndiye anayeweza kuunda akaunti za wasaidizi wa admin, watoa huduma na timu za eneo. Wasaidizi hawawezi kuunda wafanyakazi wengine wala kutoa funguo za wateja. Watoa huduma wanaweza kutoa funguo lakini hawawezi kuona orodha ya wateja. Superuser na wasaidizi wa admin wanaweza kuona historia ya ukaguzi na kupakua data.
6. Tumia **Navigate · Google Maps** au programu nyingine ya ramani kwenye rekodi ya mteja. Kiungo hutuma viwianishi pekee. Mpigie mteja kuthibitisha ikiwa eneo halina uhakika. Marekebisho ya pini huhifadhiwa pamoja na akaunti ya mfanyakazi na muda.
7. Wafanyakazi wa timu ya eneo huona kazi walizopangiwa pekee. Fungua kazi na uguse **Check my position against this pin** kwa kulinganisha eneo mara moja. Kivinjari kitaomba ruhusa ya eneo baada ya kugusa kitufe; ukurasa utaonyesha umbali wa makadirio na usahihi ulioripotiwa na kifaa. Kipimo hiki hakithibitishi uwepo wa mtu na eneo la mfanyakazi halihifadhiwi. Sahihisha pini baada ya kuangalia mlango wa biashara; marekebisho huhifadhiwa kwenye kumbukumbu ya ukaguzi.
8. Wasaidizi wa admin na superuser wanaweza kutumia **Delete customer** chini ya wasifu. Thibitisha kwa makini: kufuta huondoa wasifu, eneo, ridhaa na ziara, huzima funguo zilizounganishwa na simu, na hakuwezi kutenduliwa. Ikiwa rekodi nyingine ya mteja inatumia simu hiyo hiyo, tatua kwanza utata huo. Kumbukumbu za ukaguzi na kesi zilizowekwa alama huhifadhiwa bila wasifu wa mteja; usiweke taarifa binafsi zisizo za lazima kwenye maelezo ya kesi.

**MFA na urejeshaji:** Usanidi wa TOTP kwa QR/ufunguo wa mkono, misimbo ya urejeshaji iliyohifadhiwa kama heshi na inayotumika mara moja, uundaji upya, kikomo/kufunga baada ya majaribio mabaya, kumbukumbu za ukaguzi, na uthibitishaji wa kipindi/API vimetekelezwa kwenye tawi hili na vina majaribio ya SQLite. PostgreSQL na kivinjari havijathibitishwa. Mmiliki wa urejeshaji wa MFA lazima ateuliwe na `MFA_RECOVERY_OWNER_EMAIL` iwekwe kabla ya akaunti ya mfanyakazi kuanzishwa upya; API ya kuanzisha upya inakataa ombi ikiwa thamani hiyo haipo. Tovuti ya Django `/admin/` ni ya superuser pekee na inahitaji kipindi kilekile cha wafanyakazi kilichothibitishwa. Tawi hili bado halijathibitishwa kuwa tayari kwa uzalishaji.
